Privacy Policy

Shop Scheduler, a private application of JT's Auto Service
Last updated: October 28, 2026

What this is

Shop Scheduler is an internal appointment-scheduling tool used only by the staff of JT's Auto Service. It runs on a computer at the shop. It is not offered to the public, there are no customer accounts, and nothing about it is open to visitors of this website.

What information it holds

The application keeps the shop's own business records: customer names and phone numbers, vehicle details, appointments and notes. These records are stored on the shop's computer and are used only by shop staff to run the business.

How it uses Google

The application makes a nightly backup of the shop's records. The backup is encrypted on the shop's computer before it leaves it, and the encrypted file is then saved to the shop's own Google Drive. To do this, the owner of the shop signs in once with the shop's Google account and allows the application to use the drive.file permission.

  • That permission lets the application see and manage only the files it created itself. It cannot open, read, or change any other file in the Google Drive.
  • The application reads no other information from the Google account.
  • Because the backups are encrypted before they are uploaded, their contents cannot be read in Google Drive.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer any information handled by the application to anyone. The application contains no advertising and no analytics, and it does not send information to third parties other than saving the encrypted backup to the shop's own Google Drive.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and removing data

The shop keeps its backups until they are replaced by newer ones. The shop owner can remove the application's access at any time from the Google account's security settings, and can delete the backup files directly from Google Drive.

Changes to this policy

If the way the application uses information changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
