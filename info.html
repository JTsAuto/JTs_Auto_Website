FAQs & Info

What are your hours?

We're open Monday through Thursday, 10am to 6pm, and closed Friday through Sunday.

What work do you do?

A wide range, from oil changes to engine replacements. See the services page for the full list.

What don't you work on?

We don't service German vehicles, and we don't do tires (replacement, patching or mounting), alignments, body work, heater cores or glass replacement.

How do I drop off my car?

Please take your car key off your key ring first. If you're dropping off after hours, put your key in the secure drop box between the blue bay doors, with a yellow tag showing your name and phone number.

Do you accept third-party or extended warranties?

No, we don't accept third-party or extended warranties.

Anything else?

Call us at (865) 227-9000 and we'll be happy to help.
